# Archived Experiments

`numbered/` contains the superseded root-level experiments and their associated
tests. They are preserved for provenance, not maintained as current entry points.
The archived scripts may require their original data paths, model caches, and
runtime; do not assume they run from a fresh clone.

`numbered/exp64_average_palette_poles.py` reaggregates Exp63 settings by a
directional mean and is superseded by later descriptor-tail calibration.

The current implementation, active descriptors, frozen Exp81 calibration, and
supported tests are in [`../semantic_isp/`](../semantic_isp/README.md).
